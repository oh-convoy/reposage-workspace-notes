# S03 — Mobile Engineering Squad

- **Division:** Software Engineering (ENG)
- **Mission:** Build and ship the iOS and Android applications
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Product Engineering

## 1. Purpose

The Mobile Engineering Squad owns the company's iOS and Android applications from concept to release. It designs, builds, and maintains the mobile codebases, delivers a consistent and high-quality experience across both platforms, and keeps the apps performant, secure, and up to date. The squad exists so that mobile remains a first-class product surface that customers can rely on every day.
