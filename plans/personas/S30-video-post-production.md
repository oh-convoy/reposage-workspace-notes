# S30 — Video Post-Production Squad

- **Division:** Video & Audio Production (VAP)
- **Mission:** Edit, color, and finish video to broadcast quality
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP, Video & Audio Production

## 1. Purpose

The Video Post-Production Squad takes raw footage and shapes it into finished, broadcast-ready programming. It owns the edit, the grade, and the final finishing pass that turn a cut into a deliverable, and it holds the technical standards that keep every master compliant and consistent. The squad exists to protect creative intent while guaranteeing that what ships is technically sound and on-spec.
