# S06 — DevOps & Site Reliability Squad

- **Division:** Software Engineering (ENG)
- **Mission:** Automate delivery and keep production reliable, observable, and fast
- **Headcount:** 7 personas
- **Reports to:** Director of Platform Engineering

## 1. Purpose
The DevOps & Site Reliability Squad owns the path from code commit to production and the health of the systems running there. It builds the automation, pipelines, and platform tooling that let product teams ship quickly and safely, and it guards reliability, observability, and performance across the estate. The squad sets the standards for operational excellence and provides the shared foundation the rest of engineering builds upon.
