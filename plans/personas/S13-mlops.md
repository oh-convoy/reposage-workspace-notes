# S13 — MLOps Squad

- **Division:** Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- **Mission:** Industrialize the ML lifecycle: data, training, serving, monitoring
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Artificial Intelligence Officer (CAIO)

## 1. Purpose

S13 turns machine learning from isolated experiments into a dependable industrial capability. The squad owns the end-to-end lifecycle — data preparation, training, deployment, serving, and monitoring — and provides the shared platforms and guardrails that product teams build on. Its goal is to make model delivery fast, repeatable, observable, and safe at scale.
