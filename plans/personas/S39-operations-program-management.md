# S39 — Operations & Program Management Squad

- **Division:** Corporate & Shared Services (COR)
- **Mission:** Run the company's programs, process, and operations
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Operating Officer

## 1. Purpose
The Operations & Program Management Squad runs the company's cross-functional programs and keeps day-to-day operations predictable. It owns the operating rhythm, standardizes how work flows across teams, and tracks delivery against company priorities. The squad turns strategy into coordinated execution and gives leadership a reliable view of progress and operational health.
