# S10 — LLM & Agent Engineering Squad

- **Division:** Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- **Mission:** Build the LLM, agent, and orchestration capabilities in the products
- **Headcount:** 7 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of AI Engineering

## 1. Purpose

The LLM & Agent Engineering Squad turns the company's AI research into product-grade language-model and agent capabilities. It owns the model integration layer, the agent runtime, and the orchestration that lets products plan, act, and use tools reliably. The squad exists so every product team can ship intelligent, trustworthy behaviour without rebuilding the same foundations.
