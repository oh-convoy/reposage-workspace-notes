# S32 — Animation Studio Squad

- **Division:** Video & Audio Production (VAP)
- **Mission:** Create 2D/3D animated films and explainer content
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Video Production

## 1. Purpose
The squad turns scripts and ideas into finished 2D and 3D animated films and explainer content. It owns the full animation pipeline, from concept and design through animation, rendering, and delivery. It keeps visual quality and story consistency high while making animated production repeatable at scale.
