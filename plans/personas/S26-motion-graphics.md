# S26 — Motion & Graphics Squad

- **Division:** Multimedia & Design (MMD)
- **Mission:** Produce motion graphics, animation, and marketing graphics
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Design Officer

## 1. Purpose
The squad turns ideas into moving and static visual assets that carry the brand across every surface. It owns motion graphics, animation, and marketing graphics end to end, from concept to delivery-ready output. It keeps visual quality consistent and production repeatable as demand scales.
