# S07 — Quality Engineering Squad

- **Division:** Software Engineering (ENG)
- **Mission:** Protect quality with test strategy, automation, and release gates
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Engineering

## 1. Purpose

The Quality Engineering Squad owns how the company defines, measures, and defends software quality. It sets the test strategy and standards, builds the automation and tooling that make quality repeatable, and operates the release gates that keep defects out of production. The squad exists to give every product team confidence in what they ship and to make quality a shared, engineered property rather than an afterthought.
