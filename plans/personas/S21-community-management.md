# S21 — Community Management Squad

- **Division:** Social Media (SOC)
- **Mission:** Grow and care for the community across channels
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Social Media

## 1. Purpose

The Community Management Squad owns the relationship between the company and the people who gather around it. It nurtures conversation, recognition, and belonging so that members stay engaged, advocate for the brand, and bring others in. The squad exists to keep the community healthy, informed, and growing across every channel where it lives.
