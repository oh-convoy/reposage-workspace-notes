# S27 — 3D, AR & VR Squad

- **Division:** Multimedia & Design (MMD)
- **Mission:** Build 3D assets and immersive AR/VR experiences
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Immersive Media

## 1. Purpose

The squad owns everything three-dimensional and immersive: the 3D asset pipeline and the augmented and virtual reality experiences built on top of it. It turns concept art and product intent into optimized models, environments, and real-time interactions that run smoothly across headsets, mobile devices, and the web. The squad exists to keep the company's immersive surfaces visually compelling, performant, and consistent with the wider brand.
