# S24 — Product Design (UX) Squad

- **Division:** Multimedia & Design (MMD)
- **Mission:** Research users and design end-to-end product experiences
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Design Officer

## 1. Purpose
The squad owns how the product feels and flows for the people who use it. It studies user needs and behaviour, then turns those insights into end-to-end experience designs that span research, interaction, interface, and content. By keeping evidence at the centre of design decisions, it helps the company ship products that are usable, accessible, and worth returning to.
