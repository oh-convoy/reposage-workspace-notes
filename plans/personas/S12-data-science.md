# S12 — Data Science Squad

- **Division:** Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- **Mission:** Turn data into insight, forecasts, and decisions
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Data & AI Officer

## 1. Purpose

The Data Science Squad turns the company's data into insight, forecasts, and decisions. It owns the analytical models that explain what happened, predict what comes next, and recommend what to do. The squad partners with business and product teams to make evidence-based choices, and it sets the standards for experimentation, measurement, and model quality across the organization.
