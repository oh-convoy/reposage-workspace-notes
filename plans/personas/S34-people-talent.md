# S34 — People & Talent Squad

- **Division:** Corporate & Shared Services (COR)
- **Mission:** Hire, onboard, grow, and retain the people
- **Headcount:** 7 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief People Officer

## 1. Purpose
The People & Talent Squad owns the full employee journey, from attracting and hiring talent through onboarding, development, engagement, and retention. It safeguards the company's culture, keeps people practices fair and consistent, and ensures the organisation has the capability it needs to deliver on its goals. The squad is the central partner for every team on all matters relating to its people.
