# S29 — Video Production Squad (Live Action)

- **Division:** Video & Audio Production (VAP)
- **Mission:** Plan, shoot, and capture live-action video
- **Headcount:** 7 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Video Production

## 1. Purpose

The Video Production Squad (Live Action) exists to turn creative briefs into finished live-action footage. It owns planning, cinematography, lighting, sound, and on-set execution for every shoot the company runs. The squad safeguards production quality and safety while keeping schedules and budgets on track.
