# S38 — Customer Success & Support Squad

- **Division:** Corporate & Shared Services (COR)
- **Mission:** Onboard, support, and retain customers
- **Headcount:** 7 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Customer Officer

## 1. Purpose
The Customer Success & Support Squad owns the customer relationship after the sale, from first onboarding through ongoing support and renewal. It exists to make customers productive quickly, resolve their issues reliably, and keep them successful enough to stay and grow with the company. The squad is the customer's voice inside the organization, feeding insight back into product, sales, and service decisions.
