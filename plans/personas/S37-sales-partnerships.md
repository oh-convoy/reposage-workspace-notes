# S37 — Sales & Partnerships Squad

- **Division:** Corporate & Shared Services (COR)
- **Mission:** Win customers and build channel partnerships
- **Headcount:** 7 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Revenue Officer

## 1. Purpose

The Sales & Partnerships Squad owns revenue growth: it finds, wins, and expands customer relationships and builds the channel partnerships that extend the company's reach. It translates market demand into closed deals and durable commercial relationships. The squad exists to make revenue generation a repeatable, partner-supported engine rather than a series of isolated efforts.
