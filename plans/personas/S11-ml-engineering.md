# S11 — Machine Learning Engineering Squad

- **Division:** Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- **Mission:** Train, evaluate, and serve production ML models
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of AI Engineering

## 1. Purpose

The Machine Learning Engineering Squad turns models and methods into reliable production systems. It owns the full lifecycle of production models, from training and evaluation through deployment, serving, and monitoring at scale. The squad exists so that machine learning reaches customers as dependable, measurable capability rather than one-off experiments.
