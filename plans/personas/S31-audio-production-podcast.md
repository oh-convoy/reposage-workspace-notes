# S31 — Audio Production & Podcast Squad

- **Division:** Video & Audio Production (VAP)
- **Mission:** Record, edit, and publish audio and podcasts
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Video & Audio Production

## 1. Purpose

The Audio Production & Podcast Squad owns the end-to-end audio pipeline, from studio recording through editing, mixing, and publishing. It exists to deliver consistent, high-quality sound across shows, series, and campaign audio while keeping production schedules predictable. The squad sets the audio quality bar and the production standards the wider division follows.
