# S28 — Brand & Identity Design Squad

- **Division:** Multimedia & Design (MMD)
- **Mission:** Design and govern the corporate identity system
- **Headcount:** 5 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Design Officer (CDO)

## 1. Purpose
The Brand & Identity Design Squad owns the corporate identity system end to end. It defines the visual and verbal foundations — logo, typography, color, imagery, motion, and voice — and keeps them coherent across every product, channel, and market. The squad also governs how the identity is applied, so that brand consistency holds as the organization and its portfolio grow.
