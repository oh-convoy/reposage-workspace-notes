# S19 — Brand & Communications Squad

- **Division:** Digital Marketing (MKT)
- **Mission:** Own the brand narrative, voice, and public communication
- **Headcount:** 5 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Marketing Officer

## 1. Purpose
The squad safeguards and advances how the company is perceived everywhere it speaks. It shapes the brand narrative, defines the voice that carries it, and steers public communication across earned, owned, and paid channels. By aligning message, tone, and timing, it keeps the brand coherent and trusted as the company grows.
