# S20 — Social Strategy Squad

- **Division:** Social Media (SOC)
- **Mission:** Set social channel strategy, calendars, and campaigns
- **Headcount:** 5 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Social Media

## 1. Purpose

The Social Strategy Squad defines how the company shows up across every social channel. It sets channel-level strategy, owns the editorial calendar, and shapes the campaigns that carry brand and product messages to the right audiences. The squad exists to give social activity a clear direction, a consistent cadence, and a measurable link to business goals.
