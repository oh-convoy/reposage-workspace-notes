# S16 — Content Marketing Squad

- **Division:** Digital Marketing (MKT)
- **Mission:** Produce the editorial content that attracts and educates the market
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Digital Marketing

## 1. Purpose

The Content Marketing Squad owns the editorial voice that draws the market in and helps it understand what the company does. It plans, writes, and maintains the articles, guides, and campaign copy that build awareness and trust across every channel. The squad exists to turn market insight and product knowledge into credible content that earns attention and moves buyers forward.
