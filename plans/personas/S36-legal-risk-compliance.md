# S36 — Legal, Risk & Compliance Squad

- **Division:** Corporate & Shared Services (COR)
- **Mission:** Protect the company legally and keep it compliant
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Legal & Compliance Officer

## 1. Purpose
The Legal, Risk & Compliance Squad safeguards the company's legal standing, manages enterprise risk, and ensures adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and internal policies. It provides the governance backbone that lets the rest of the organization move quickly without taking on unacceptable exposure. The squad translates regulatory obligations into practical guidance and monitors the risk landscape so issues are surfaced and addressed early.
