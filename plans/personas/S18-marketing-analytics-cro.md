# S18 — Marketing Analytics & CRO Squad

- **Division:** Digital Marketing (MKT)
- **Mission:** Measure marketing, attribute results, and optimize conversion
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

## 1. Purpose

The Marketing Analytics & CRO Squad makes marketing performance measurable and improvable. It owns attribution, marketing reporting, and the experimentation practice that turns traffic into revenue. The squad gives channel owners trustworthy numbers and a disciplined way to test, learn, and lift conversion across the funnel.
