# S25 — UI & Visual Design Squad

- **Division:** Multimedia & Design (MMD)
- **Mission:** Craft interfaces, visual systems, and design tokens
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Design Officer

## 1. Purpose

The UI & Visual Design Squad owns the visual language and interface craft that make the company's products coherent, legible, and pleasant to use. It defines the visual systems and design tokens that keep every screen consistent, and it shapes the interfaces through which users experience the product. The squad exists to translate brand and product intent into a durable, reusable visual foundation.
