# S33 — Music & Sound Design Squad

- **Division:** Video & Audio Production (VAP)
- **Mission:** Compose music and design sound for all media
- **Headcount:** 5 personas
- **Reports to:** VP, Video & Audio Production

## 1. Purpose
The squad owns the sonic identity of everything the company publishes, from original scores to final mixes. It composes music and designs sound that carry emotion and meaning across video, audio, and interactive media. It keeps audio quality consistent, rights-clear, and delivery-ready as output scales.
