# S17 — SEO & Organic Search Squad

- **Division:** Digital Marketing (MKT)
- **Mission:** Grow durable organic search visibility and traffic
- **Headcount:** 5 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Digital Marketing

## 1. Purpose

The SEO & Organic Search Squad owns the company's unpaid search presence across every market it serves. It makes our content and properties findable, trusted, and competitive in search engines so that organic channels become a durable source of demand. The squad exists to turn search visibility into measurable, compounding business value rather than one-off traffic spikes.
