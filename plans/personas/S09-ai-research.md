# S09 — AI Research Squad

- **Division:** Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- **Mission:** Advance the company's core AI methods and publish the science
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of AI Research

## 1. Purpose

The AI Research Squad advances the company's fundamental AI capabilities by developing new methods, models, and evaluation practices. It sets the scientific direction for machine learning across the organization and translates early research into foundations that product teams can build upon. The squad also owns the company's research reputation, publishing peer-reviewed work and engaging with the wider scientific community.
