# S22 — Creator & Influencer Partnerships Squad

- **Division:** Social Media (SOC)
- **Mission:** Source, contract, and run creator partnerships
- **Headcount:** 5 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Social Media

## 1. Purpose

The Creator & Influencer Partnerships Squad builds and manages the network of creators who carry the brand into their own audiences. It finds the right voices, agrees terms, and runs partnerships that feel authentic to the creator and valuable to the company. The squad exists to turn creator relationships into a durable, measurable channel rather than one-off campaigns.
