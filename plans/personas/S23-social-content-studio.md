# S23 — Social Content Studio Squad

- **Division:** Social Media (SOC)
- **Mission:** Produce the native short-form content each platform rewards
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Social Media

## 1. Purpose

The Social Content Studio Squad exists to make the company fluent in the native language of every social platform. It turns brand and campaign direction into short-form video, posts, and visuals built for the way each audience actually scrolls. The squad owns the creative craft, the production cadence, and the platform-specific formats that earn reach and engagement.
