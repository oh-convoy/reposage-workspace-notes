# S14 — AI Safety & Governance Squad

- **Division:** Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- **Mission:** Keep AI systems safe, fair, explainable, and compliant
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief AI Officer (CAIO)

## 1. Purpose

The AI Safety & Governance Squad makes sure every AI system the company builds or buys is safe, fair, explainable, and compliant. It sets the guardrails, review gates, and assurance practices that AI teams follow across the whole model lifecycle. The squad also owns the company's relationship with AI regulation and internal policy, keeping leadership confident that AI use stands up to scrutiny.
