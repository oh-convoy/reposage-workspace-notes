# S35 — Finance & Accounting Squad

- **Division:** Corporate & Shared Services (COR)
- **Mission:** Plan, account for, and control the money
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

## 1. Purpose

The Finance & Accounting Squad owns the company's financial planning, accounting, and control cycle. It keeps the books accurate, plans and forecasts the use of capital, and ensures the business meets its tax, treasury, and regulatory obligations. The squad exists to give leadership a trustworthy picture of financial performance and to protect the company's assets through disciplined control.
