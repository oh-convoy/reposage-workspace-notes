# S05 — Data Engineering Squad

- **Division:** Software Engineering (ENG)
- **Mission:** Build the pipelines, storage, and data platform the company runs on
- **Headcount:** 6 personas
- **Reports to:** VP of Engineering

## 1. Purpose

The Data Engineering Squad owns the company's data platform end to end: the pipelines that move data, the storage that holds it, and the services that make it usable. The squad turns raw operational signals into trustworthy, well-modelled datasets that analysts, product teams, and machine learning systems can rely on. It sets the standards for data quality, reliability, and cost, and it evolves the platform as volume and demand grow.
