# S15 — Growth & Performance Marketing Squad

- **Division:** Digital Marketing (MKT)
- **Mission:** Acquire and retain users through measurable growth programs
- **Headcount:** 7 personas
- **Reports to:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

## 1. Purpose

The Growth & Performance Marketing Squad owns the company's user acquisition and retention engine. It plans, runs, and measures paid and organic growth programs across channels, turning marketing spend into predictable, attributable results. The squad also runs the experimentation and lifecycle programs that keep users engaged after the first conversion. Everything it does is grounded in data, so leadership can see what growth costs and what it returns.
